## UAE public holidays 2027 at a glance

Fixed dates (New Year's Day and National Day) are set by law. Everything marked "Expected" follows the Hijri calendar and can shift by a day once the crescent moon is sighted.

The biggest unknown is Eid Al Fitr. If Ramadan lasts 29 days, the break is expected to run Tuesday to Thursday, March 9–11. If it lasts 30 days, it is expected to extend to Friday, March 12, which turns it into a five-day break once you add the weekend.

## Why Eid dates are always estimates

The Islamic calendar is lunar, so each month begins when the new crescent moon is sighted. That is why every Hijri holiday moves about 11 days earlier each Gregorian year.

The UAE moon-sighting committee meets on the evening before a possible start date. Government announcements usually follow within days, sometimes only a day or two before the holiday itself.

In practice, the estimates published a year ahead are rarely off by more than one day. Plan around them, but keep refundable bookings for anything tied to Eid.

## How holiday moves work in the UAE

Since 2024, the Cabinet can shift most public holidays to the beginning or end of the week. The idea is to give residents longer weekends instead of a single day off in the middle of the week.

There are three limits. Eid Al Fitr and Eid Al Adha are never moved. A holiday is not moved if it overlaps another public holiday. And a holiday that already lands on a Saturday or Sunday is not moved to a weekday.

The 2026 Prophet's Birthday shows how this works. The occasion itself was expected on Tuesday, August 25, but the official day off was moved to Friday, August 28, creating a three-day weekend.

In 2027, both Hijri New Year (expected Sunday, June 6) and the Prophet's Birthday (expected Saturday, August 14) are projected to fall on weekend days. If those estimates hold, they will not create an extra weekday off.

## Public sector vs private sector

Federal government entities and private companies usually announce the same holiday, but the length can differ. Government staff hear from the Federal Authority for Government Human Resources, while private sector staff hear from the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation.

The 2026 Eid Al Adha break is a good example. Private sector employees were off Tuesday to Friday, May 26–29. Federal government staff were also off on Monday, May 25, which gave them nine days in a row with the weekends on both sides.

Private sector employees who wanted the same nine days could request one leave day for that Monday. Whether that works depends on your contract and your manager's approval.

## Planning travel around UAE holidays

Flights out of Dubai and Abu Dhabi get expensive around both Eids and National Day. Many residents travel at the same time, so fares and hotel rates rise as soon as the dates are confirmed.

Book early with free cancellation, then adjust once the official dates are announced. A one-day shift rarely matters for hotels, but it can matter for non-refundable flights.

If you stay in the country, staycation packages tend to sell out quickly for the four-day National Day weekend. Beach hotels and desert resorts usually fill first.
